Not imported on purpose: Anthropic batch / fast mode / data-residency 1.1x / tool-use fees, Gemini cache-storage fees and
future-dated prices, regional uplifts. A usage record naming such a tier is left unpriced, never priced at the standard rate.
Adapter fixes that the prices depend on: Anthropic prompt tokens now include cache reads/writes (tier threshold counts them);
Gemini thinking tokens are added to output (they are billed as output).
